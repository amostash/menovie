Meet Your Physician

Poroshat Shekarloo, MD

OB/GYN · NAMS-Certified Menopause Practitioner · Founder, Menovie Health

Dr. Shekarloo is a practicing Obstetrician-Gynecologist, global health advocate, and NAMS Certified Menopause Practitioner.

She began her career as a community activist working on domestic and sexual violence and substance abuse in communities of color, before completing residency at Northshore LIJ and joining Maimonides Medical Center, where she has practiced for over a decade as an attending hospitalist, gynecological surgeon, Director of Global Health, and Director of Peer Review & Patient Safety.

In 2018 she founded the Global Health Institute at Maimonides, leading surgical missions to Kenya and Guatemala with Saving Mothers, where she also serves on the medical board.

In 2025, driven by her own challenging journey through perimenopause, she launched Menovie Health to close a gap she saw clearly from both sides of the exam table: menopause care that treats the whole woman, not just her hormone panel.
